Parent questions

The four things parents ask first.

There's no catch. This is a Grade 11 IB student's Creativity, Activity, Service (CAS) project — David Hendrix Marbella, an IB student at Riffa Views International School in Bahrain, is personally funding the materials, tools, and coordination. The ₱200 covers only your child's rover kit and your family's snacks on August 1. The rest is his contribution to the Nuvali community.
None. Scratch is the same block-based platform MIT designed for ages 8–16. There's no typing of code — just connecting colorful blocks. Each group has a dedicated IB student facilitator guiding 4 students through every activity.
ASPN charges a ₱150 parking fee at the gate for non-residents. Your child needs to bring nothing — every kit component is pre-sorted and waiting at their assigned table. Refreshments are provided. Just arrive between 12:00 and 1:00 PM for on-site registration.
Each session builds on the previous one, so we encourage full attendance. If your child misses one, our facilitator team and parent support community will help them catch up before the next Saturday. Missing two or more sessions makes the August 1 showcase harder to participate in fully.
